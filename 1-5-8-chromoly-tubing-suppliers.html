1-5/8 Chromoly Tubing Suppliers

1-5/8 inch 4130 Chromoly Tubing is widely used in motorsport chassis fabrication, aerospace structures, roll cages, bicycle frames, aircraft components, and high-strength mechanical assemblies. The material offers an excellent combination of strength, toughness, weldability, and weight reduction compared to conventional carbon steel tubing.

Siddhgiri Overseas supplies 1-5/8 Chromoly Tubing in seamless and welded forms with various wall thicknesses, cut lengths, and heat treatment conditions. Material is available with EN 10204 3.1 certification and can be supplied for domestic and export projects.
