4130 Chrome Moly Pipe Suppliers

4130 Chrome Moly Pipe is a high-strength low-alloy steel pipe widely used in aerospace, motorsport, oil and gas, hydraulic systems, structural engineering, and industrial fabrication projects. The addition of chromium and molybdenum improves strength, toughness, wear resistance, and hardenability while maintaining excellent weldability and machinability.

Siddhgiri Overseas supplies ASTM A519 AISI 4130 Chrome Moly Pipes in seamless and welded forms with complete material traceability and EN 10204 3.1 certification. Our stock range includes multiple diameters, wall thicknesses, and custom cut lengths suitable for demanding engineering applications.

AISI 4130 alloy steel pipes are preferred for applications requiring a high strength-to-weight ratio, excellent fatigue resistance, and dependable performance under pressure and elevated temperatures. These pipes are extensively used in racing chassis, aircraft structures, drilling equipment, pressure systems, and industrial machinery.
