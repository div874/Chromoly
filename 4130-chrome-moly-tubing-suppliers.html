4130 Chrome Moly Tubing Overview

4130 Chrome Moly Tubing is a chromium-molybdenum low-alloy steel tubing widely used in industries that require a combination of high strength, toughness, weldability, and weight reduction. The material offers excellent mechanical properties and is commonly selected for demanding structural and engineering applications.

The addition of chromium improves hardenability and wear resistance, while molybdenum enhances strength, toughness, and elevated temperature performance. These characteristics make AISI 4130 tubing suitable for aerospace structures, motorsport fabrication, hydraulic systems, mechanical components, and industrial equipment.
