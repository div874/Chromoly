Chemical Composition of 4130 Chromoly Pipe

4130 Chromoly Steel derives its performance from a carefully balanced chemical composition. Chromium improves wear resistance and hardenability, while molybdenum enhances strength and high-temperature performance. Carbon contributes to strength, and manganese improves toughness and hardenability.

The combination of these alloying elements creates a versatile material suitable for structural, mechanical, aerospace, and industrial applications requiring dependable performance and long service life.

AISI 4130 Chromoly Pipe is recognized internationally through several equivalent standards and designations used across engineering and manufacturing industries worldwide.

Mechanical Properties of 4130 Chromoly Pipe

4130 Chromoly Pipe offers an excellent combination of strength, ductility, toughness, and fatigue resistance. These properties make it suitable for applications where lightweight construction and mechanical reliability are important.

The material performs well under dynamic loading conditions and can be heat treated to achieve desired strength levels. Its balance of weldability and mechanical performance makes it a preferred choice for demanding engineering projects.
