4130 Chromoly Material Characteristics

4130 Chromoly is valued for its combination of strength, toughness, weldability, and fatigue resistance. The material is widely selected where reliable performance and long service life are required.

The alloy responds well to fabrication, machining, and heat treatment processes, making it suitable for a wide variety of industrial applications. Its excellent strength-to-weight ratio allows engineers and fabricators to achieve performance objectives while maintaining efficient structural design.
