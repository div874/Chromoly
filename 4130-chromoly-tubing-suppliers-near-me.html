4130 Alloy Tubing Overview

4130 Alloy Tubing is a chromium-molybdenum low alloy steel tubing widely used in aerospace, motorsport, automotive, fabrication, and industrial engineering applications. Commonly known as 4130 Chromoly Tubing, the material offers an excellent combination of strength, toughness, weldability, and durability.

AISI 4130 Chemical Composition

AISI 4130 derives its performance from a balanced combination of carbon, chromium, molybdenum, manganese, and silicon. Chromium improves wear resistance and hardenability, while molybdenum contributes to strength and elevated temperature performance.

The controlled alloy composition provides an excellent balance of strength, toughness, weldability, and fatigue resistance, making 4130 Chromoly suitable for demanding industrial and engineering applications.

Element Role in Material Performance
CarbonContributes to strength and hardness.
ChromiumImproves wear resistance and hardenability.
MolybdenumEnhances strength and toughness.
ManganeseImproves hardenability and durability.
SiliconSupports strength and deoxidation.
IronForms the primary base metal.
