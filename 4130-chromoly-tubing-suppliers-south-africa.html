AISI 4130 Alloy Information

AISI 4130 is a low alloy chromium-molybdenum steel that combines strength, toughness, and weldability. Due to its low carbon content, the material offers good fabrication characteristics while maintaining excellent mechanical performance.

The alloy exhibits good atmospheric corrosion resistance and dependable fatigue strength, making it suitable for demanding operating environments where durability and reliability are important.

4130 Chromoly Tubing is widely used in applications requiring lightweight construction, high strength, and long service life.
