4130 Alloy Steel Properties

4130 Chromoly is a low alloy steel containing chromium and molybdenum that provides an excellent combination of strength, toughness, and weldability. The material is widely used in applications requiring high mechanical performance and reliable structural integrity.

The alloy offers good fatigue resistance, wear resistance, and heat treatment capability, making it suitable for demanding industrial and engineering environments.

Its excellent strength-to-weight ratio allows engineers and fabricators to achieve lightweight yet durable designs across a wide range of applications.
