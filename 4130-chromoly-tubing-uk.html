4130 Alloy Steel Properties

4130 steel is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel commonly referred to as Chromoly steel or CrMo steel. The material offers an excellent combination of strength, toughness, and weight efficiency.

Compared with conventional carbon steels, 4130 Chromoly provides improved mechanical performance and structural reliability. Its excellent fatigue resistance and heat treatment capability make it suitable for demanding applications.

The alloy is widely used where lightweight construction and high mechanical strength are required.
