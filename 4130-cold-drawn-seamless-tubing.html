4130 Cold Drawn Seamless Tubing

Siddhgiri Overseas supplies premium quality 4130 Cold Drawn Seamless Tubing manufactured for demanding engineering and industrial applications. Produced using chromium-molybdenum alloy steel, these tubes provide excellent mechanical properties, dimensional accuracy, and long service life.

4130 Cold Drawn Seamless Tubing is widely recognized for its high strength-to-weight ratio, weldability, toughness, and fatigue resistance. The seamless manufacturing process eliminates weld joints and enhances structural integrity for critical applications.

The cold drawing process improves surface finish, dimensional precision, and mechanical performance, making the tubing suitable for precision engineering, aerospace components, motorsport fabrication, hydraulic systems, and structural applications.
