4130 Steel Suppliers

Siddhgiri Overseas is a trusted supplier of premium quality 4130 alloy steel products manufactured to meet demanding industrial applications. AISI 4130 steel is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel widely recognized for its excellent strength, toughness, weldability, and fatigue resistance.

4130 steel offers an exceptional combination of mechanical performance and lightweight properties, making it suitable for structural engineering, aerospace components, motorsport fabrication, oil and gas systems, and precision manufacturing applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AISI 4130 steel?

AISI 4130 is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel known for its high strength, toughness, and excellent weldability.

Is 4130 steel weldable?

Yes. AISI 4130 can be welded using appropriate welding procedures and heat treatment when required.

What industries use 4130 steel?

Industries such as aerospace, motorsport, oil and gas, fabrication, and engineering commonly use 4130 steel.
