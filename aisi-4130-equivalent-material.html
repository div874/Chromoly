What is AISI 4130 steel?

AISI 4130 is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel known for its high strength, toughness, and weldability.

Is AISI 4130 suitable for welding?

Yes, AISI 4130 offers good weldability when appropriate welding procedures are followed.
