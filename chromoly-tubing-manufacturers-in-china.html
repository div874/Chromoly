Features of 4130 Chromoly Tubing

4130 chromoly tubing is recognized for its excellent balance of strength, toughness, and weight reduction. Chromium and molybdenum alloying elements improve hardenability and mechanical performance while maintaining good weldability and machinability.

The material offers outstanding fatigue resistance and structural stability under dynamic loading conditions, making it suitable for high-performance engineering applications. Its excellent strength-to-weight ratio allows designers and fabricators to reduce overall component weight without sacrificing structural integrity.

  • High strength-to-weight ratio
  • Excellent weldability
  • Superior fatigue resistance
  • Good machinability
  • High toughness and durability
  • Excellent hardenability
  • Reliable impact resistance
  • Long service life

Manufacturing Process of 4130 Tubing

4130 chromoly tubing can be produced through hot finished and cold drawn manufacturing processes depending on application requirements. Cold drawn tubing provides improved dimensional accuracy, smoother surface finish, and tighter tolerances.

Advanced heat treatment methods such as annealing, normalizing, quenching, and tempering are used to achieve desired mechanical properties. These processes enhance toughness, strength, and overall material performance.
