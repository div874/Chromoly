4130 Chromoly Properties

4130 alloy steel is known for its excellent strength-to-weight ratio and outstanding mechanical performance. The addition of chromium improves corrosion resistance and hardenability, while molybdenum enhances strength at elevated temperatures.

The material exhibits excellent weldability, machinability, and fatigue resistance, making it suitable for structural and dynamic loading applications.

  • Excellent weldability
  • Superior fatigue resistance
  • Good machinability
  • Outstanding toughness
  • Excellent hardenability
  • Reliable impact resistance
  • Long service life
