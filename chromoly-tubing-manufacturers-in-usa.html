About 4130 Chromoly Tubing

4130 chromoly tubing is a low-alloy steel containing chromium and molybdenum that provides an excellent combination of strength, toughness, and weldability. The material is widely used where lightweight construction and high mechanical performance are required.

Siddhgiri Overseas supplies premium quality 4130 tubing manufactured according to international standards for demanding industrial applications. The alloy exhibits excellent fatigue resistance and dimensional stability under severe service conditions.

4130 alloy steel tubing is extensively used in aerospace structures, roll cage fabrication, motorsport assemblies, hydraulic systems, oil and gas equipment, and industrial machinery.

Available in seamless and welded forms, 4130 tubing provides long service life, reliable performance, and excellent fabrication characteristics.

Manufacturing and Processing

4130 chromoly tubing can be manufactured through hot finished and cold drawn processes. Cold drawn seamless tubing offers tighter tolerances, smoother surface finish, and enhanced mechanical consistency.

Heat treatment processes such as annealing, normalizing, quenching, and tempering improve strength and toughness for specialized applications.
