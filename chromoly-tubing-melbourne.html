4130 Chromoly Properties

4130 chromoly steel offers an exceptional balance of strength, toughness, and weight reduction. Chromium improves hardenability and wear resistance, while molybdenum enhances mechanical performance under demanding service conditions.

  • Excellent Strength-to-Weight Ratio
  • Superior Toughness
  • Excellent Weldability
  • Good Machinability
  • High Fatigue Resistance
  • Heat Treatable Material
  • Reliable Impact Resistance
  • Long Service Life
  • Consistent Mechanical Performance
  • Dimensional Stability

Cold Drawn Seamless Tubing Benefits

Cold drawn seamless tubing provides improved dimensional accuracy, superior surface finish, and uniform mechanical properties. The seamless construction eliminates weld joints and ensures consistent strength throughout the tube.

These properties make cold drawn 4130 tubing highly suitable for precision engineering, aerospace structures, roll cages, and high-performance industrial applications.
