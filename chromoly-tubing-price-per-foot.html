4130 Chromoly Properties

4130 chromoly steel is a low-alloy chromium-molybdenum steel offering excellent mechanical performance and durability. Its unique chemical composition delivers a combination of strength, toughness, and machinability suitable for demanding applications.

  • Excellent Strength-to-Weight Ratio
  • High Fatigue Resistance

  • Reliable Structural Performance
  • Dimensional Stability
  • Long Service Life

AISI 4130 Chemical Composition

AISI 4130 contains controlled quantities of carbon, chromium, molybdenum, manganese, and silicon. Chromium improves hardenability and wear resistance while molybdenum enhances strength and high-temperature performance.

AISI 4130 Mechanical Properties

The alloy exhibits excellent tensile strength, yield strength, and elongation characteristics. These properties make 4130 tubing suitable for high-load structures, safety-critical components, and dynamic applications requiring fatigue resistance.
