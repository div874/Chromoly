4130 Chromoly Tube Specifications

4130 chromoly tubing is supplied in seamless, welded, and cold drawn conditions. The alloy combines chromium and molybdenum to provide high strength, improved hardenability, and excellent fatigue resistance.

  • Material Grade: AISI 4130 / SAE 4130
  • UNS Number: G41300
  • Supply Condition: Seamless, Welded, Cold Drawn
  • End Finish: Plain End, Beveled End
  • Certification: EN 10204 3.1 MTC
  • Inspection: PMI, Hardness and Mechanical Testing
  • Available Forms: Round, Square and Rectangular Tubes
