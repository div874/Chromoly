What is 4130 Chromoly Tubing?

4130 Chromoly Tubing is a low-alloy steel tubing containing chromium and molybdenum as primary alloying elements. It is widely recognized for its exceptional strength-to-weight ratio, high toughness, good fatigue resistance, and excellent weldability. Due to these characteristics, 4130 chromoly tubing is extensively used in demanding applications requiring lightweight yet high-strength materials.
