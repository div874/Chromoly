Frequently Asked Questions

What is Chromoly Tubing?

Chromoly tubing is a chromium-molybdenum alloy steel tubing known for its high strength, excellent toughness, and lightweight properties. SAE 4130 Chromoly is widely used in aerospace and motorsport industries.

Is 4130 Chromoly tubing weldable?

Yes, 4130 Chromoly tubing offers excellent weldability when proper welding procedures and filler materials are used.

What standards does Chromoly tubing comply with?

Chromoly tubing is commonly supplied according to ASTM A519, ASME SA519, SAE 4130, and AISI 4130 standards.

Do you provide seamless Chromoly tubing?

Yes, seamless Chromoly tubing is available in various diameters and wall thicknesses for high-performance applications.

Are custom cut lengths available?

Yes, tubing can be supplied in custom cut lengths based on project requirements.

What certifications are provided?

Material Test Certificates (MTC 3.1), dimensional reports, and third-party inspection documents are available.

What industries use Chromoly tubing?

Chromoly tubing is widely used in aerospace, motorsport, automotive, industrial fabrication, and structural engineering sectors.

Why is 4130 Chromoly preferred for roll cages?

4130 Chromoly provides an excellent strength-to-weight ratio, allowing strong yet lightweight roll cage structures.
