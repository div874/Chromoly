Specifying the Wall Thickness

At 0.500" OD, wall thickness selection governs the bore dimension, weight per foot, and structural efficiency. Common wall thicknesses for 4130 CDS in this OD range include 0.035", 0.049", 0.065", 0.083", 0.095", and 0.120" — though availability for specific combinations must be confirmed at the enquiry stage. Heavier walls provide more cross-sectional area for axial loads; thinner walls reduce weight and increase the bore for hydraulic or instrumentation applications.

Application Considerations

At 0.500" OD, 4130 tubing is commonly used in structural framing, motorsport structures, aerospace sub-assemblies, and instrumentation or hydraulic lines depending on wall selection. The OD determines the tube notching and tube-end preparation for welded joints — all tube-to-tube welds at this OD must be notched or fishmouth-cut to the mating tube's OD.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 0.500" OD 4130 tubing available from stock?

Common 0.500" OD / wall combinations are typically more readily available than less common sizes. Confirm specific wall availability at the RFQ stage — not all OD/wall combinations are held in stock at all times.

What wall thickness should I choose for this OD?

Wall selection depends on the application's load requirements, weight budget, and bore requirement. Structural analysis or application-specific guidelines should determine the minimum wall for the design load. Consult the applicable rulebook for motorsport applications.
