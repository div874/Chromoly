Pipe vs. Tube Sizing

This distinction is fundamental in industrial procurement:

- Pipe (NPS/NB system): Sized by nominal bore. A 14" NPS pipe has an OD of 14.000" — but for smaller NPS sizes, the OD does not correspond to the NB at all (e.g., 1" NPS pipe has OD of 1.315").
- Mechanical tubing (4130 CDS): Sized by actual OD and wall in decimal inches or millimetres. There is no 'nominal bore' designation.

4130 chromoly CDS tubing is a mechanical tube product — it is specified by OD and wall, not by NB/NPS. If you are looking for a large-diameter 4130 tube, specify the required actual OD (in inches or mm) and wall.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is large-diameter 4130 tubing (e.g., 14" OD) available?

The verified OD range for standard supply is 0.25" to 5.0". OD dimensions larger than 5.0" require a special enquiry and are not standard catalogue items. For requirements above 5" OD, contact the supplier with the required OD, wall, and application for a feasibility assessment.
