Aerospace Specification Context

The term 'aerospace' implies the most stringent interpretation of every specification element. In aerospace procurement for 4130 tubing:

- The applicable standard is typically AMS-T-6736 or an approved material specification listed in the aircraft type design
- The condition is Normalized unless the type design specifies otherwise
- MTC documentation must explicitly state compliance with the applicable specification and condition
- Third-party inspection (EN 10204 3.2 or customer source inspection) may be required for some programmes
- Material traceability (heat number, lot, batch) must be maintained through the supply chain

Documentation Hierarchy

In aerospace procurement, the documentation hierarchy matters. Understanding the distinction:

- EN 10204 3.1: MTC certified by the manufacturer's own authorised representative. Accepted for most general aviation and MRO applications.
- EN 10204 3.2: MTC also witnessed and countersigned by an independent inspection organisation or the buyer's representative. Required for some OEM and defence programmes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EN 10204 3.1 MTC acceptable for aerospace 4130 tubing?

For most general aviation MRO and EAB construction, EN 10204 3.1 MTC is accepted. For OEM or defence programmes, the specific programme requirements determine the required documentation level — some programmes require 3.2 or customer source inspection. Confirm with your quality manager.
