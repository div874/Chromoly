When 4130 is Appropriate for Capillary Applications

Most instrumentation capillary tubing is stainless steel (for corrosion resistance) or instrument steel with specific bore requirements. 4130 chromoly capillary tube is an uncommon product. If a capillary-range tube is required in 4130, this is likely because: a specific strength requirement at fine bore dimension is needed; or the application is within an existing 4130-alloy assembly and material consistency is required.

Buyers should submit an enquiry with the exact OD, wall, and bore requirements for a feasibility and availability assessment.

Specification for Very Small OD Tube

For small-diameter tube, dimensional tolerances become proportionally larger relative to the tube cross-section. Specifying the exact OD tolerance, wall tolerance, and bore smoothness requirements is essential for very small tube. The applicable standard and inspection requirements should also be explicitly stated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is stainless steel typically used instead of 4130 for capillary applications?

Yes, in most instrumentation and medical capillary applications, stainless steel (e.g., 316SS) is standard due to its corrosion resistance. 4130 capillary tube would be a specialist requirement.
