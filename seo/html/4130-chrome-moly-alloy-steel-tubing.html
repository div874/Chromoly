4130 Chrome Moly Alloy Steel Tubing

4130 chrome moly alloy steel tubing refers to tubing produced from 4130 chromium-molybdenum alloy steel. For procurement, the material designation should be accompanied by the required tubing form, outside diameter, wall thickness, material condition, applicable specification and documentation requirements.

B2B Sourcing for Chrome Moly Alloy Tubing

4130 chrome moly alloy steel tubing is not a commodity stocked in general steel service centres. It is a specialist alloy mechanical tube product sourced from specialist distributors who maintain the necessary MTC documentation infrastructure. The sourcing process operates on a Request for Quotation (RFQ) model: the buyer submits a complete specification; the supplier returns a formal quotation valid for a defined period; the buyer issues a purchase order; the material is confirmed and despatched with MTC.

What Affects Lead Time

Lead time for 4130 chrome moly alloy tubing depends on whether the required dimension is held in stock or requires a mill order. Common OD and wall combinations within the verified range (OD 0.25" to 5.0", wall 0.035" to 0.500") are more likely to be available from stock with shorter lead times. Uncommon dimensions, very large volumes, or specific condition requirements (such as normalized to AMS-T-6736) may require a mill order with correspondingly longer lead times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to specify 'alloy steel' on a purchase order for 4130?

The grade designation '4130' (AISI/SAE) is itself sufficient to identify the alloy. However, including 'alloy steel' in the product description helps internal material classification systems and receiving inspection procedures correctly categorise the incoming material.
