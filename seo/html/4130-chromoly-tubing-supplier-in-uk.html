Supply Specifications and Quality

Supplying the UK's motorsport valley and advanced manufacturing sector with MTC-documented 4130 tubing, ensuring compliance with European and global engineering standards. All standard 4130 tubing supplied is Cold Drawn Seamless (CDS) to ASTM A519, ensuring tight dimensional tolerances suitable for precision fabrication, machining, and welding. Hot Finished Seamless (HFS) is available for heavy-wall requirements.

Documentation and Traceability

Every delivery is accompanied by an EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificate (MTC). This documents the ladle analysis (chemical composition) and heat number, providing full traceability from the steel mill to your receiving dock. This documentation is essential for ISO 9001, AS9100, and motorsport technical inspection compliance.
