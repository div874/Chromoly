4130 Cold Drawn Seamless Tubing

Seamless 4130 tubing is manufactured without a longitudinal weld seam, offering uniform mechanical properties across the cross-section. This product form is required for high-stress structural and aerospace applications where isotropic strength and fatigue resistance are critical.

ASTM A519 — The Governing Standard

In North America, 4130 Cold Drawn Seamless tubing is governed by ASTM A519 (Standard Specification for Seamless Carbon and Alloy Steel Mechanical Tubing). The standard covers both CDS and HFS forms, specifying chemical composition, dimensional tolerances, permissible variations, and mechanical property requirements (where applicable). Grade 4130 is listed explicitly in ASTM A519.

For aerospace applications, AMS-T-6736 (Aircraft Structural Tubing, Chromium-Molybdenum Steel) governs and mandates the normalized condition alongside the seamless form.

Dimensional Tolerances

ASTM A519 specifies tolerances for CDS 4130 tubing based on OD and wall. Buyers should consult the current version of the standard for the applicable tolerance class for their specific dimension. Key points:

- OD tolerance is a percentage of nominal OD for most size ranges.
- Wall thickness tolerance under ASTM A519 CDS is typically expressed as a percentage overage/underage.
- Tolerances tighten with smaller OD; looser tolerances apply to larger diameters.
- For dimensions requiring tolerances tighter than standard, a precision-drawn specification is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between 4130 CDS and 4130 precision tubing?

Standard CDS 4130 per ASTM A519 is manufactured to standard dimensional tolerances. 'Precision tubing' implies tighter tolerances than standard, typically requiring additional drawing passes or a precision-drawn specification. Precision tubing should be specified with the required OD and wall tolerances, not just the 'precision' label.

Is AMS-T-6736 CDS 4130 available in the same OD range as standard ASTM A519?

AMS-T-6736 covers a defined range of sizes for aircraft structural applications. The size range may differ from the full ASTM A519 range. Verify availability for your specific dimension with the supplier at the enquiry stage.
