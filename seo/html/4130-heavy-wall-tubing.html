Applications for Heavy-Wall 4130

Heavy-wall 4130 is used in: thick-wall cylinder bodies (machined from tube stock); roll cage main hoops in heavier vehicle classes requiring larger cross-section; hydraulic actuator tubes; heavy structural columns and frame members; and machined flanges and collars where the tube wall provides the machining stock for the finished profile.

Hot Finished vs. Cold Drawn for Heavy Wall

For very heavy walls (typically above 0.250"), Hot Finished Seamless (HFS) may be more practical than CDS, particularly for larger ODs. The cold drawing process becomes progressively more demanding as wall-to-OD ratio increases. For heavy-wall requirements, confirm whether CDS or HFS is available for the specific OD/wall combination at the RFQ stage.
