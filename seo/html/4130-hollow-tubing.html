Tube vs. Bar in 4130 Alloy

4130 alloy is available in two primary product forms: hollow tube and solid bar (rod). When a buyer specifies '4130 hollow tubing', they are confirming the hollow product form — not the solid bar that is also used as machining stock. The hollow tube in 4130 is the CDS seamless tube governed by ASTM A519. Solid 4130 bar is governed by ASTM A29.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 4130 hollow tubing the same as 4130 bar with a hole bored through it?

No. 4130 CDS hollow tube is produced by the seamless tube manufacturing process (billet piercing and drawing) — not by boring a solid bar. Boring a solid bar to create a hollow section produces different grain flow and dimensional characteristics, and is not the standard tube product.
