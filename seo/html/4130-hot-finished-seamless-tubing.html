Product Form

4130 Hot Finished Seamless Tubing

Seamless 4130 tubing is manufactured without a longitudinal weld seam, offering uniform mechanical properties across the cross-section. This product form is required for high-stress structural and aerospace applications where isotropic strength and fatigue resistance are critical.

When HFS is the Right Choice

Hot Finished Seamless 4130 is appropriate when:
- The required OD is in the larger range where cold drawing equipment limits apply
- The application is heavy machining from solid tube wall, where the surface scale will be removed during machining and close tolerances on the as-received tube are less critical
- The application specifies or tolerates the broader dimensional tolerances characteristic of hot-finished product

For precision structural, aerospace, and thin-wall applications, Cold Drawn Seamless (CDS) remains the appropriate form.

Surface and Tolerance Differences

HFS tubing has a characteristic scaled, oxidised surface finish from the elevated-temperature processing. This scale must be removed by machining, sandblasting, or other means before welding or finishing. Dimensional tolerances are broader than CDS — wall thickness variation and OD tolerance are less precise in HFS form. Buyers specifying HFS for precision applications should confirm the tolerance class with the supplier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I weld 4130 HFS tubing without removing the scale?

No. Mill scale on hot-finished steel must be removed from the weld zone before welding. Scale contamination produces porosity, inclusions, and weakened welds. Grinding or machining the weld area is required.
