Why Seamless Matters for Hydraulic Service

In hydraulic tubing, internal pressure loads are transmitted through the tube wall as hoop stress. The stress concentration created by a longitudinal weld seam — even in high-quality DOM tubing — represents a potential initiation site for fatigue cracking under cyclic pressure loading. Seamless 4130 CDS tubing eliminates this seam, providing uniform hoop stress distribution around the entire circumference.

Important Note on Pressure Ratings

ASTM A519 mechanical tubing is not specifically pressure-rated under a vessel code. If a hydraulic line must comply with a pressure vessel code (such as ASME B31.3 for pressure piping), the design engineer must calculate the allowable working pressure based on the tube dimensions, material properties, applicable code, and design temperature. Do not assume that any stated wall thickness automatically provides a specific working pressure without an engineering calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 4130 tubing suitable for aircraft hydraulic lines?

4130 CDS tubing is used for aircraft hydraulic lines, but the application must be engineered to the applicable aircraft standards (e.g., MIL-H-5440, relevant FAA/EASA requirements). The specific OD, wall, fitting, and installation requirements are determined by the aircraft type design or MRO authority.

Can I use schedule-based sizing for 4130 hydraulic tubing?

4130 mechanical tubing is sized by OD and wall thickness — not by pipe schedule. Schedule designations (Schedule 40, 80, etc.) apply to pipe (NPS nominal bore system), not mechanical tubing. Specify OD and wall for all 4130 mechanical tube requirements.
