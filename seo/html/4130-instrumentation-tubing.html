Specification for Instrumentation Use

Instrumentation tubing requires especially tight dimensional control. The OD must be consistent for fitting compatibility (instrumentation fittings are designed to specific OD tolerances). Wall thickness must be uniform to ensure consistent pressure capacity. Surface finish of the bore may be important for certain measurement applications.

For 4130 instrumentation tubing, specify: exact OD with tolerance class, wall thickness with tolerance, applicable standard, condition, surface finish if relevant, and MTC documentation.

Stainless vs. 4130 for Instrumentation

In most general process instrumentation, stainless steel (316L or similar) is standard due to its corrosion resistance in fluid measurement lines. 4130 alloy is appropriate for instrumentation within high-strength structural systems — such as aircraft instrument lines within a 4130 airframe structure — where the same alloy family is specified throughout for consistency and the environment is controlled.

Frequently Asked Questions

What fitting standards are compatible with 4130 instrumentation tubing?

Compression fittings (e.g., Swagelok, Parker, or equivalent) are designed to specific OD tolerances. Confirm that the 4130 tubing OD tolerance is compatible with the fitting specification before ordering. The fitting manufacturer's dimensional requirements should be consulted.
