Large OD Supply Considerations

At larger ODs (above approximately 3"), fewer wall combinations are typically held in stock. The OD/wall combination must be confirmed at the RFQ stage. Large-OD 4130 is more often sourced on a per-order basis rather than from readily available stock. HFS may be the only practical form for the heaviest combinations.

Applications for Large-OD 4130

Large-diameter 4130 is used in: heavy structural frames and column structures; large-bore cylinder bodies machined from tube stock (actuators, hydraulic cylinders); drive axle housings; industrial machine frames; and custom engineering projects requiring alloy steel in large section.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is large-diameter 4130 tubing available from stock?

Common sizes in the 2"–4" OD range may be available from stock. Larger or less common OD/wall combinations typically require a mill order. Confirm availability and lead time at the RFQ stage.
