Mechanical Tubing vs. Pressure Tubing

The distinction between mechanical and pressure tubing is important for procurement:

- Mechanical tubing (ASTM A519): Designed for structural, mechanical, and engineering applications. Tolerances and testing are calibrated for dimensional accuracy and mechanical property consistency. Not designed or tested for pressure containment service.

- Pressure tubing (ASTM A519 is sometimes used, but dedicated pressure tube standards such as ASTM A213 are more appropriate for high-pressure applications): Designed to contain internal pressure — additional testing requirements may apply.

For roll cages, chassis, aircraft airframes, and similar structural applications, mechanical tubing per ASTM A519 is the correct product.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can ASTM A519 mechanical tubing be used in pressure systems?

ASTM A519 mechanical tubing is not specifically designed or tested for pressure containment service. For pressure-critical applications, the applicable pressure vessel or pressure tubing standard should be specified (e.g., ASTM A213 for boiler/heat exchanger tubes). Verify requirements with the responsible engineer.
