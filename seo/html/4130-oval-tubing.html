Where Oval Tube Is Used

Oval tube has application in bicycle design (top tubes and downtubes where aerodynamic profiling and reduced lateral bending resistance are desired), and in specialist automotive bodywork components. However, these applications typically use aluminium alloy or mild steel, not 4130 chromoly.

For 4130 Requirements

If a design calls for an oval cross-section in 4130, the options are: fabricating from round tube by mechanically flattening (which significantly changes mechanical properties and is not recommended for structural applications); machining from solid bar stock; or reviewing the design to determine whether round tube of equivalent section modulus meets the engineering intent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can round 4130 tubing be formed into an oval shape?

Mechanically crushing or pressing round tube into an oval shape work-hardens and often buckles or cracks the tube wall. This is not an acceptable manufacturing method for structural applications. Any non-circular cross-section from 4130 must be designed from the outset, not formed from round stock.
