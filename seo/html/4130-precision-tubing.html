What 'Precision' Means in Specification

There is no single universal definition of 'precision tubing'. When a buyer specifies 'precision 4130 tubing', they should provide the specific dimensional tolerances required, not just the label. Precision tubing procurement typically specifies:
- OD tolerance (e.g., ±0.001" rather than standard percentage tolerance)
- Wall tolerance (e.g., ±5% rather than standard ±10%)
- Eccentricity (maximum allowed wall-thickness variation around the circumference)

Without explicit tolerance requirements, the supplier cannot determine whether standard CDS tolerances suffice or whether an additional drawing pass is required.

Applications for Precision 4130 Tube

Precision 4130 is used in applications including: instrumentation tube where the bore must accept a defined fitting consistently; hydraulic lines where OD-to-fitting fit is critical; and precision machined components where the tube is used as machining stock and the starting OD tolerance directly affects machining allowance and finished-dimension accuracy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is standard ASTM A519 CDS considered precision tubing?

Standard CDS 4130 per ASTM A519 has defined tolerance classes that are tighter than HFS but not as tight as the most demanding precision applications. For many structural applications, standard CDS tolerances are sufficient. For instrumentation and tight-fit assemblies, verify the specific tolerance required.
