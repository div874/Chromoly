How Rulebooks Determine Size

Almost all motorsport regulations mandate larger diameter and thicker wall tubing as vehicle weight or speed increases. Furthermore, different parts of the cage have different requirements:
- Main Hoop & A-Pillar: Require the heaviest, largest tubing as they protect the driver envelope in a rollover.
- Door Bars / Side Impact: Often match the main hoop or are slightly smaller.
- Rear Braces & Secondary Diagonals: May be permitted to use smaller OD or thinner wall tubing to save weight.

Frequently Asked Questions

If the rules say 1.50 x 0.083 for 4130, can I use 1.50 x 0.083 mild steel instead?

No. Most rulebooks require thicker walls if you choose to build with mild steel (DOM) rather than 4130. For example, a rule might allow 1.50x0.083 for 4130, but demand 1.50x0.120 for mild steel. The MTC 3.1 is required to prove you used 4130.
