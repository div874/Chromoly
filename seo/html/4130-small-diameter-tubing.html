Verified Small-OD Range

The verified minimum OD in the standard supply range is 0.25" (6.35mm). At small ODs, the wall-to-OD ratio is higher for a given wall thickness, meaning the tube is proportionally thicker relative to its size. This is actually an advantage for many applications — small 4130 tubes can carry significant loads relative to their light weight.

Dimensional Accuracy at Small OD

CDS dimensional tolerances expressed as a percentage of OD become physically small at fine sizes. Small-OD tube may require specific tolerance class confirmation for fitting compatibility. State OD tolerance requirements explicitly at the enquiry stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the smallest OD available in 4130 CDS?

The verified minimum OD in the standard supply range is 0.25" (6.35mm). For ODs smaller than this, the product becomes capillary tube — a specialist product with different enquiry requirements.
