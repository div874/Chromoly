Engineering Considerations for Thin Wall

Thin-wall tube design involves: column buckling (thinner walls have lower moment of inertia for compressive members); local buckling (very thin walls relative to OD may buckle before the material yields — check OD/t ratio against design guidelines); welding heat input (thin walls are more susceptible to weld distortion and burn-through — use reduced amperage and faster travel speed); and handling damage (thin-wall tube is more susceptible to denting during storage and transport).

Thin Wall vs. Minimum Wall

Note the distinction: 'thin wall' is a design choice (the designer chooses a light wall for weight saving); 'minimum wall' is a tolerance convention (ASTM A519 allows wall to be below nominal by a specified percentage). A thin-wall tube to ASTM A519 may have an actual wall at the minimum wall tolerance — buyers should clarify whether the nominal or minimum wall is the design limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the thinnest wall available in 4130 CDS tubing?

The verified minimum wall in the standard supply range is 0.035". Walls below this are specialty products that require a custom enquiry. For 0.035" wall, confirm availability for your specific OD at the RFQ stage.
