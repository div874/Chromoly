Engineering Significance of the Wall

At 0.083" wall, the tube cross-sectional area (and therefore load-carrying capacity in tension/compression) is determined by the OD selected alongside this wall. Thinner walls (0.083" is in the thinner range for most ODs) provide lighter tubes at the cost of reduced section area. Thicker walls increase section area, weight, and cost. The minimum required wall is typically determined by the structural design calculation for the member load case.

Typical Applications

The 0.083" wall 4130 CDS tube is used in applications where a balance of light weight and adequate structural capacity is required. The 0.083" wall is widely used in primary roll cage members and main chassis rails. At most common ODs (1.0"–1.75"), this wall provides substantial section capacity for typical motorsport loads.

Frequently Asked Questions

What OD can I combine with 0.083" wall?

The 0.083" wall is available across a range of ODs within the verified supply range (0.25"–5.0" OD). Smaller OD tubes with 0.083" wall have a relatively thick wall-to-OD ratio; larger OD tubes with 0.083" wall are thinner-walled relative to their size. Confirm the specific OD/wall combination at the RFQ stage.
