4130 Tubing Chemical Composition

Procurement of 4130 Tubing Chemical Composition requires exact specification of outside diameter, wall thickness, and material condition. Applicable standards determine the mechanical properties and certification requirements for this chromium-molybdenum alloy.

The Role of the Alloying Elements

- Carbon (C): At ~0.30%, it provides sufficient hardenability and strength without making the steel excessively brittle or unweldable.
- Chromium (Cr): Adds strength, hardness, and slightly improves resistance to scaling at moderate temperatures. (Note: at ~1%, it is not enough to make the steel 'stainless').
- Molybdenum (Mo): Increases high-temperature strength, hardenability, and improves the steel's toughness and fatigue resistance.
- Manganese (Mn): Acts as a deoxidizer during steelmaking and contributes to strength.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find the exact composition of the tubing I purchase?

The exact composition of the specific batch of steel used to make your tubing is found under the 'Ladle Analysis' or 'Heat Analysis' section of the EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificate provided with your delivery.
