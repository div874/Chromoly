How Custom Sizes are Achieved

Custom sizes are achieved at the manufacturing mill through the Cold Drawn Seamless (CDS) process. By drawing a standard 'mother tube' through a custom-sized external die and over a custom-sized internal mandrel, the mill can dictate the exact OD and wall thickness (and thereby the ID) to tight tolerances.

Requirements for Custom Sourcing

Because custom sizes require dedicated setup, tooling, and a specific production run, they operate under different commercial rules than stock inventory:
- Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ): Custom mill runs require significant volume, often measured in thousands of feet or by the ton.
- Extended Lead Times: Custom runs must be slotted into the mill's production schedule, meaning lead times are measured in months, not days.
- Tooling Costs: Depending on the mill, there may be upfront tooling charges if the required dies and mandrels do not already exist.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order just 20 feet of a custom size?

Generally, no. Custom dimensions require a new mill production run. Producing only 20 feet is economically impossible due to setup costs and material waste. For small quantities, engineers must usually design around standard, available catalog sizes.
