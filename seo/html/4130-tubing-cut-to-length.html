Advantages of Pre-Cut Supply

1. Reduced Shipping Costs: Tubing cut under certain lengths (often 4-8 feet) can be shipped via standard parcel carriers (UPS/FedEx) rather than expensive commercial freight trucks.
2. Zero Scrap: You pay for and receive exactly what your BOM requires. You don't pay to store or dispose of unusable off-cuts.
3. Shop Efficiency: Material arrives ready for fabrication, freeing up your shop staff from rough-cutting material.

Specifying Cut Orders

When ordering cut-to-length material, precision matters. You must specify the exact cut length required and understand the cutting tolerances. Standard industrial cutting may have a tolerance of +/- 1/8". If your application requires tighter precision for immediate CNC machining, this must be specified upfront.

Frequently Asked Questions

If I buy cut-to-length tube, do I still get the MTC?

Yes. The Mill Test Certificate applies to the master heat lot. All pieces cut from that heat lot are covered by that MTC. You will receive the documentation verifying the material's properties regardless of length.
