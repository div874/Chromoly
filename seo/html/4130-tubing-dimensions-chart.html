Specifying Dimensions

When ordering globally, it is best practice to specify the exact decimal inch dimension, even if your local drawings are in metric. The manufacturing tooling for ASTM A519 CDS is typically based on inch dimensions. Converting a hard metric requirement (e.g., exactly 25.00mm) to the closest imperial size (1.000" = 25.4mm) may require design adjustments on your end.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order 4130 tubing in true metric sizes?

True metric OD sizes (e.g., exactly 40.0mm) are available from mills drawing to EN standards (like 25CrMo4 to EN 10297). Standard ASTM A519 4130 stock is almost universally drawn to inch dimensions. If you require true metric ODs, specify EN 25CrMo4 or request a custom mill run.
