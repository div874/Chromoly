Supply Chain Requirements for Aerospace 4130

Aerospace manufacturer supply chain requirements for 4130 tubing include:

- Specification compliance: AMS-T-6736 (Condition N) for structural aircraft tube. ASTM A519 for ground equipment and non-flight structures.
- MTC completeness: Must show grade, heat number, specification compliance, condition, and test results.
- First Article Inspection (FAI): Some programmes require FAI documentation for first deliveries of a new part.
- Certificate of Conformance (CoC): In addition to MTC, a CoC signed by the supplier's quality representative may be required.
- Counterfeit material controls: Aerospace QSPs require controls to detect potentially counterfeit material. Sourcing from reputable, documented supply chains with full MTC traceability mitigates this risk.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between MTC and Certificate of Conformance for aerospace?

The MTC (Mill Test Certificate) documents actual test results from the steel mill — chemical analysis, mechanical properties. The Certificate of Conformance (CoC) is a supplier declaration that the material meets the specified requirements. Aerospace quality plans often require both. The MTC provides evidence; the CoC provides the formal quality declaration.
