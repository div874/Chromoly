Aerospace Supply Chain Requirements

Aerospace buyers ordering 4130 tubing typically operate within a quality management framework (AS9100, NADCAP-adjacent requirements, or customer-specific quality plans). Supply chain requirements include:

- Full first-article documentation
- Heat-lot traceability throughout the supply chain
- MTC at EN 10204 3.1 minimum (3.2 for some programmes)
- Chemical analysis and mechanical testing results on MTC
- Certified compliance with applicable specification (AMS-T-6736 for aircraft structural tube)
- Packaging and handling to prevent surface damage or contamination

Non-Aircraft Aerospace Applications

For ground support equipment, test stands, and other non-flight structures, the specification requirements may differ from flight-article structures. These applications often accept ASTM A519 Grade 4130 rather than AMS-T-6736, with appropriately documented MTC. The responsible quality engineer must confirm which specification applies to each item within the programme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do aerospace programmes require EN 10204 3.2 MTC?

It depends on the programme. Some OEM and prime contractor programmes require 3.2 (independently witnessed) documentation; others accept 3.1. Confirm the documentation requirement with your quality plan or customer before ordering.
