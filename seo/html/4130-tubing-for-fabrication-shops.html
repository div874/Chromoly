Shop Stock vs. Project Buy

A fabrication shop buying 4130 for shop stock maintains a ready supply of the most commonly used sizes. This reduces lead time for production jobs and allows cutting to order from stock. The considerations:

- Stock sizes: Order the 3–5 OD/wall combinations used most frequently in lengths appropriate for shop storage
- MTC management: Maintain one MTC per size/heat lot — label tube bundles with heat lot reference
- Replenishment: Order in advance of stock depletion to avoid production disruption

Mixed-Size Orders

Fabrication shops often need multiple tube sizes simultaneously for a single job. Consolidated mixed-size orders simplify logistics and MTC documentation. Some suppliers can provide all sizes with matching MTC in a single delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

What length is standard for CDS 4130 tubing supply?

Standard mill lengths for CDS 4130 are typically 20 feet (approximately 6 metres), though this varies by supplier and size. Random lengths (within a range) or specific cut lengths can be arranged with suppliers. Confirm at the RFQ stage.
