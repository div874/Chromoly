Why 4130 for Hydraulic Lines

In a hydraulic system, the tube wall carries internal pressure as hoop stress. Higher material yield strength allows thinner walls at the same design pressure — reducing the tube weight and the volume of hydraulic fluid in the system (which affects response time). 4130 seamless provides this strength advantage over mild steel DOM or ERW tube. The seamless construction also eliminates the longitudinal seam as a potential fatigue-initiation site under cyclic pressure loading.

System Design Considerations

Designing 4130 hydraulic tubing into a system requires the engineer to address:

- Wall thickness: Calculated from the design pressure, OD, safety factor, and material allowable stress per the applicable code (e.g., ASME B31.3, SAE J1677)
- Bending: Tube must be bent to route around obstacles; minimum bend radius must be respected to avoid wall thinning or cracking
- Fitting compatibility: OD tolerance of the tube must match the fitting specification (Parker, Swagelok, JIC, or other)
- Vibration: Clamping and routing must prevent resonance at system operating frequencies

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 4130 tubing rated for a specific hydraulic pressure?

No. Pressure capacity is not a fixed property of a tube — it depends on the wall thickness, OD, material condition, applicable safety factor, and design code. The hydraulic system engineer must calculate allowable working pressure for each tube size in the circuit.
