Motorcycle Frame Applications

A motorcycle tube frame carries the engine, transmission, suspension loads, and rider weight. The primary structural elements are:

- Backbone / spine: The main structural member connecting headstock to swingarm pivot
- Downtube: Front frame member from headstock, often carrying the engine
- Seat loop / rear subframe: Lighter structure supporting seat and tail
- Engine mounts: Gusseted tube structures that react engine torque and vibration

Custom Frame Material Considerations

For custom motorcycle frames intended for road use, the responsible builder must ensure the design and construction meet the applicable road vehicle regulations in the jurisdiction. Many countries require type approval or inspection of custom frames before road registration. Material certification (MTC) may be requested by inspection authorities as part of the homologation process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an MTC for a custom motorcycle frame build?

MTC is good practice for any structural build. For road-legal custom motorcycle registration in many jurisdictions, an inspection authority may request material certification. Retain the MTC with your build documentation regardless of whether it is immediately required.
