Professional Fabricator Supply Requirements

Professional motorsport fabricators require:

1. Consistent dimensions: Production benders and notching templates are set to specific ODs. Tubes with OD variation outside CDS tolerances cause fit-up problems and rework.
2. Surface quality: MTC-documented CDS 4130 with bright surface. Scaled or corroded tube increases weld prep time.
3. MTC availability: Tech inspectors at professional events expect MTC to be available. Some series require MTC for approval of cage constructions.
4. Cut-to-length options: Pre-cut tube reduces shop waste and cutting time for high-volume builds.
5. Mixed-size single orders: Professional shops use many OD/wall combinations simultaneously. Consolidated orders reduce paperwork and delivery complexity.

Tube Kit Supply

Some motorsport fabricators supply tube kits to amateur builders — pre-cut 4130 to a specific cage design. For kit suppliers, MTC provision with each kit is important for the end-builder's tech inspection compliance.
