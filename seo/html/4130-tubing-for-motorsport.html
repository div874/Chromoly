The Motorsport Buyer's Priorities

Motorsport buyers — whether professional fabricators or amateur builders — share a set of priorities when sourcing 4130 tube:

1. Grade confidence: The material must genuinely be 4130 — not mild steel relabelled. The MTC confirms this.
2. Seamless construction: Most rules prohibit welded tubing for safety structures. CDS confirms no longitudinal seam.
3. Dimensional accuracy: The OD must be consistent for tube notching and fitting. CDS tolerances provide this.
4. MTC availability: Tech inspectors may request material certification. A supplier who cannot provide MTC is unsuitable for motorsport supply.
5. Cut-to-length: Many builders prefer to receive cut lengths to minimise workshop waste.

Documentation for Tech Inspection

At scrutineering, the tech inspector may ask to verify that the roll cage material is 4130 chromoly. The MTC is the standard means of proof. Retain the MTC with the vehicle logbook for the life of the cage structure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information does a motorsport MTC need to show?

At minimum: material grade (AISI 4130), product form (seamless), dimensions (OD and wall), the heat/cast number, and the ladle analysis. Mechanical test results may also be required depending on the specification. Keep the full MTC — do not rely on a summary sheet.
