OEM Supply Requirements

For OEM production purchasing, the key supply requirements are:

1. Dimensional consistency: Each delivery must meet the same OD/wall tolerance so that production tooling (benders, notchers, fixtures) does not require re-setting between deliveries.
2. Heat-to-heat consistency: Different heat lots may exhibit minor property variations within the specification. For tight machining tolerances, request that deliveries remain from the same heat lot where possible.
3. MTC continuity: Each delivery should come with its own MTC. The OEM quality system should file these by part number and delivery batch.
4. Supply security: For production-critical parts, discuss safety stock and lead time expectations with the supplier.

Integrating 4130 into the QMS

For OEMs operating under ISO 9001, AS9100, or IATF 16949, incoming material inspection and traceability are mandatory. The MTC provides the material traceability reference. Each incoming delivery of 4130 should be: received against the purchase order, MTC checked against the PO requirements, accepted or rejected by incoming QC, and stored with heat lot identification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a supplier provide 4130 tubing in a consistent heat lot across multiple deliveries?

For standard commercial sizes, a supplier may be able to reserve a specific heat lot for sequential deliveries. For smaller volumes, this is less practical as heat lots are consumed. Discuss with the supplier at RFQ stage if heat-lot consistency is a QMS requirement.
