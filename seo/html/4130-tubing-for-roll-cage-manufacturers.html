Production Safety Responsibility

Roll cage manufacturers take on product liability for the structures they build. A cage constructed from incorrectly specified or documented material cannot be defended in a post-incident investigation. The MTC is the primary evidence that the correct material was used — both for the builder's own protection and for tech inspection compliance.

Supply Priorities for Roll Cage Manufacturers

Key priorities for a roll cage manufacturer's 4130 supply:

1. MTC for every delivery: Non-negotiable. Every bundle of tube must be traceable to a documented heat lot.
2. Consistent OD dimensions: Production tube notching, fishmouth jigs, and benders are set to specific ODs. Material with OD variation produces poor fits requiring rework or rejection.
3. Multiple size capability: Most cage designs use 2–4 different OD/wall combinations. Order all sizes from a single supplier for documentation simplicity.
4. Tube condition: As-drawn CDS is standard for roll cage production. Normalized (AMS-T-6736) is required for FAA-certificated aircraft work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should roll cage manufacturers provide MTC copies to their customers?

Yes — providing MTC copies with completed cages is good practice and increasingly expected by professional race teams and sanctioning bodies. Some series require MTC at tech inspection. Providing documentation differentiates a professional manufacturer from a backyard builder.
