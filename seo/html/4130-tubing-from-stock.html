Standard Stock vs. Mill Orders

Stock inventory covers the most widely used dimensions in the motorsport, aerospace, and structural engineering sectors. This typically includes ODs from 0.500" to 2.500" in common structural walls (0.049" to 0.120"). Material is held in standard random mill lengths (usually 17-24 feet) and in standard conditions (typically As-Drawn or Normalized).

Speed and Logistics

Ordering from stock allows for rapid turnaround. Once the order is confirmed, the material can be picked, cut (if required), securely packaged, and dispatched to the freight carrier. All stock material maintains full traceability, meaning the MTC 3.1 is immediately available upon dispatch.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the specific wall thickness I need is not in stock?

If your exact specification is not in stock, you have two options: substitute an available wall thickness (requires engineering approval, usually moving to the next heavier wall), or place a mill order if the project timeline and volume permit.
