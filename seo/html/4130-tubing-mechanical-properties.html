Key Mechanical Definitions

- Yield Strength: The stress point at which the tube begins to deform plastically (permanently). This is the critical number for structural chassis design.
- Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS): The maximum stress the material can withstand before breaking.
- Elongation: A measure of ductility; how much the material stretches before fracture.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the MTC show the exact mechanical properties of my tubing?

If mechanical testing was specified on the order (or mandated by the standard, like AMS-T-6736), the MTC will show the actual tested Yield, Tensile, and Elongation values for that specific heat lot.
