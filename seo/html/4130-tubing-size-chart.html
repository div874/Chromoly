Understanding the Range

The verified supply range spans from 0.250" OD up to 5.000" OD, with wall thicknesses from 0.035" up to 0.500".

Not every wall thickness is manufactured for every OD. For example, a 0.500" wall on a 0.500" OD tube is physically impossible (it would have no bore). Conversely, very thin walls (0.035") on very large ODs (4.000") are prone to crushing and are not standard catalog items.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the ID (Inside Diameter) from this chart?

ID is calculated as: OD - (2 × Wall Thickness). For example, a 1.500" OD tube with a 0.120" wall has an ID of 1.500 - (2 × 0.120) = 1.260".
