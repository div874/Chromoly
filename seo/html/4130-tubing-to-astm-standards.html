Specifying 'ASTM Standards' Without a Specific Reference

A purchase order stating '4130 tubing to ASTM standards' without identifying the specific standard requires the supplier to infer the applicable document. The supplier will typically apply ASTM A519 for seamless tube. To avoid ambiguity and ensure the correct standard is applied, always state the specific ASTM standard number on the purchase order.

Frequently Asked Questions

What ASTM standard should I reference for 4130 aerospace tubing?

For FAA-certificated aircraft structural applications, the standard is AMS-T-6736 (not an ASTM standard). For other aerospace applications and general structural use, ASTM A519 is the primary reference. Verify the applicable specification with the design authority or aircraft inspector.
