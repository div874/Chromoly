The Weight Calculation Formula

You do not need an exhaustive chart to find the weight of any 4130 tube; you only need the standard steel tube weight formula.

Weight (lbs/foot) = (OD - Wall) × Wall × 10.69

Where OD and Wall are in decimal inches.

Example for 1.500" OD × 0.083" wall:
(1.500 - 0.083) × 0.083 × 10.69
= 1.417 × 0.083 × 10.69
= 1.25 lbs per foot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 4130 tubing lighter than mild steel tubing of the same size?

No. A 1.50" x 0.120" 4130 tube weighs virtually similar depending on specification as a 1.50" x 0.120" mild steel tube. 4130 saves weight because its higher strength allows you to design with a thinner wall (e.g., using 0.083" 4130 instead of 0.120" mild steel).
