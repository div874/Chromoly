Why Welded 4130 Is Metallurgically Problematic

Standard ERW tube welding involves forming a flat steel strip into a circular cross-section and joining the longitudinal seam by electric resistance welding — a process that heats the steel very rapidly and allows it to cool quickly. For steels with high carbon equivalent values (CEV), this rapid thermal cycle creates a heat-affected zone that is prone to forming brittle martensite.

4130's carbon (approximately 0.28–0.33%), chromium (approximately 0.80–1.10%), and molybdenum (approximately 0.15–0.25%) additions give it a carbon equivalent value that makes ERW welding of the strip problematic without significant post-weld heat treatment — which defeats the economic purpose of the process.

For this reason, 4130 chromoly is industrially produced and supplied as seamless tubing.

DOM Tubing Is Not 4130

Some buyers conflate '4130 welded' with 'DOM' (Drawn Over Mandrel) tubing. DOM tubing is typically made from 1020 or similar mild steel strip, formed and ERW welded, then drawn over a mandrel to improve concentricity and compress the weld zone. DOM is not 4130 alloy. A buyer specifying 4130 chromoly must order seamless alloy, not DOM mild steel.

If a motorsport application specifies '4130 roll cage tubing', the correct product is seamless 4130 alloy — not DOM.

What to Specify Instead

If a buyer is seeking 4130 tubing and is comparing it to DOM or ERW mild steel: specify Cold Drawn Seamless 4130 (ASTM A519). If the application can use mild steel, DOM 1020 to ASTM A513 may be specified separately. The two products are not interchangeable on a 4130-specific blueprint.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can 4130 be welded in fabrication?

Yes — welding 4130 tubing during fabrication (e.g., TIG welding a roll cage or airframe) is standard practice. This page addresses the tube manufacturing process, not the fabrication welding process. Seamless 4130 tube is TIG-welded during fabrication all the time.

Is DOM 4130 tubing a real product?

Not in the standard commercial market. DOM is a mild steel product. '4130 DOM' as a search term usually reflects a misconception. The correct 4130 product is Cold Drawn Seamless.
