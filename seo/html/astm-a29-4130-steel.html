A29 vs. A519 — Product Form Governs

The material grade (4130) is the same in both standards, but the product standard governs the product form and testing requirements:

- ASTM A29: Bar (round bar, square bar, hexagonal bar) — used as machining stock, shafting, and raw material for machined parts.
- ASTM A519: Seamless tubing — hollow circular cross-section for structural and mechanical tube applications.

The two products are not interchangeable. A purchase order referencing 'ASTM A29 4130' will produce bar, not tube.

When A29 4130 Bar Is Specified

4130 bar to ASTM A29 is used for: machined aerospace components (brackets, fittings, pins), round bar for turning into shafts or spindles, and square/hex bar for machined tooling applications. The MTC for A29 bar documents chemical analysis and mechanical properties per the standard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use ASTM A29 4130 bar as a substitute for ASTM A519 4130 tube?

No. A29 bar is solid; A519 is hollow tube. They are different product forms and cannot be substituted for each other on structural tube applications.
