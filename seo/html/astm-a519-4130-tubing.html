What ASTM A519 Covers

ASTM A519 specifies:

- Chemical composition limits for listed grades, including Grade 4130
- Product forms: Cold Drawn Seamless (CDS) and Hot Finished Seamless (HFS)
- Dimensional tolerances by form and size range
- Permissible variations in OD, wall, and straightness
- Mechanical property requirements (where applicable — these are typically not mandatory for every tube unless the buyer specifies 'mechanical properties required')
- Permitted manufacturing processes
- Marking requirements

A519 does not cover: pressure vessel ratings; corrosion resistance; elevated temperature properties; or fabrication/welding requirements — these must be addressed by the relevant engineering specifications.

Ordering to ASTM A519 — Complete Specification

A complete order specification for ASTM A519 Grade 4130 must include:

- Standard: ASTM A519
- Grade: 4130
- Form: CDS or HFS
- OD and Wall (in decimal inches or mm)
- Condition: As-drawn, Normalized, Annealed, or Stress-relieved
- Supplementary requirements (if mechanical property testing is required)
- Documentation: EN 10204 3.1 MTC

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ASTM A519 automatically require mechanical property testing?

Not by default. Under ASTM A519, mechanical properties (yield strength, tensile strength, elongation) may not be mandatory unless the buyer invokes the relevant supplementary requirement or the specific grade/condition mandates testing. If mechanical property certification is required, state it explicitly on the purchase order.

Is ASTM A519 acceptable for aerospace 4130 applications?

For FAA-certificated aircraft structural repair, AC 43.13-1B directs AMS-T-6736, not ASTM A519. For experimental aircraft and many ground equipment applications, ASTM A519 CDS 4130 in normalized condition is commonly specified. Confirm requirements with the design authority or aircraft inspector.
