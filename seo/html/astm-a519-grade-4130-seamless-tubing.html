ASTM A519 Grade 4130 Seamless Tubing

Seamless 4130 tubing is manufactured without a longitudinal weld seam, offering uniform mechanical properties across the cross-section. This product form is required for high-stress structural and aerospace applications where isotropic strength and fatigue resistance are critical.

CDS Dimensional Tolerance Reference

Under ASTM A519, Cold Drawn Seamless (CDS) 4130 tubing is subject to specific dimensional tolerances for OD and wall. For most common OD ranges, the OD tolerance is within ±0.5% of the nominal OD, and wall tolerance is typically +20% / -0% (minimum wall guarantee). Buyers with tighter tolerance requirements should state them explicitly — standard tolerances may not be sufficient for precision machined applications.

**Important:** Always refer to the current version of ASTM A519 for the specific tolerance class applicable to your OD and wall combination.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I specify ASTM A519 Grade 4130 CDS in metric dimensions?

Yes. ASTM A519 accommodates metric dimensions. State OD and wall in millimetres if working to metric drawings. The specification requirements are the same regardless of unit system.
