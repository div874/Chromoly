Grade 4130 Composition in ASTM A519

The ASTM A519 Grade 4130 composition limits are defined in the specification's chemical composition table. Buyers should refer to the current version of ASTM A519 for the precise limits. The key alloying elements and their approximate ranges in 4130 are:

- Carbon: approximately 0.28–0.33%
- Chromium: approximately 0.80–1.10%
- Molybdenum: approximately 0.15–0.25%

These are approximate ranges for general reference. The actual limits are defined in the current ASTM A519 standard. Always verify the actual ladle analysis on the MTC against the current specification limits for your specific heat of material.

**Note:** Buyers should not assume that all material sold as '4130' contains exactly these values — the MTC ladle analysis provides the actual chemistry for the specific heat supplied.

MTC Verification for Grade 4130

When receiving 4130 tubing to ASTM A519, the following should be checked on the MTC:

1. The material standard is stated as ASTM A519
2. The grade is stated as 4130
3. The ladle analysis (chemical analysis) shows element values within the A519 Grade 4130 limits
4. The heat/cast number is recorded
5. The product form (CDS or HFS) matches the order
6. The condition is recorded

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the MTC ladle analysis shows a carbon value at the upper limit?

As long as the carbon value is within the ASTM A519 Grade 4130 specified limits, the material meets the standard. Material at the upper carbon limit will have slightly higher hardenability than lower-carbon heats, which is generally not a problem for structural applications but may be relevant for welding procedure selection.
