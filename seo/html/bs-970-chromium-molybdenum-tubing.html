BS 970 and Its EN Successors

BS 970 is withdrawn for new designs in favour of the harmonised EN standard series:

- BS 970 708M40 → superseded by EN 10083 42CrMo4 / 1.7225 (closer to AISI 4140)
- BS 970 708M30 → approximated by EN 10083 30CrMo4 / 1.7228 (closer to AISI 4130 in carbon)

The AISI 4130 equivalent in Europe is 25CrMo4 (1.7218). The exact BS 970 grade on an old drawing should be identified before assuming it maps directly to 4130.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use 4130 where a BS 970 drawing specifies 708M40?

No — 708M40 corresponds more closely to 4140 (higher carbon). Substituting 4130 for a 708M40 specification requires a formal engineering comparison and approval, as the strength levels achievable in equivalent conditions differ significantly.
