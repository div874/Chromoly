What to Specify for Custom Cut Supply

For custom cut orders, specify:
- OD and wall (the tube specification)
- Required cut length(s) in inches or mm
- Cut length tolerance (±1/8" or tighter as required)
- Number of pieces at each length
- Square or miter cut end requirement (if applicable)
- Deburring requirement (specify if square cut ends must be deburred)

Cut-to-Length vs. Standard Length

Standard 4130 CDS supply is in full mill lengths (typically 20 ft / 6 m, though this varies by supplier and size). Custom cut provides specific lengths at additional cutting cost. For large projects with many cuts, custom cut supply may be more economical in total (reduced workshop labour and scrap) even if the per-foot cost is higher.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the MTC remain valid for custom-cut pieces?

Yes. The MTC is issued for the heat lot of the full-length tube. Cut pieces from that heat lot retain the same heat lot traceability. The MTC applies to all cut pieces from the same heat of material.
