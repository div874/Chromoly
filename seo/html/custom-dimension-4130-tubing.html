When Custom Dimensions are Needed

Custom dimensions are appropriate when:
- The application requires a very specific bore dimension that cannot be achieved by standard OD/wall combinations
- The design requires an OD or wall outside the standard catalogue range
- The application requires tighter tolerances than standard ASTM A519 CDS permits
- A large volume is required at a non-standard dimension that justifies a mill production run

Custom Dimension Enquiry Requirements

For custom dimension enquiries, provide:
- Required OD (actual, in decimal inches or mm)
- Required wall (actual, in decimal inches or mm) or required bore if the bore is the controlled dimension
- OD and wall tolerance class required
- Required length and total footage or weight
- Applicable standard
- Condition requirement
- Application description
- Timeline

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a minimum order quantity for custom dimension 4130 tubing?

Custom mill orders typically have minimum tonnage requirements that vary by the mill and dimension. For small quantities of non-standard dimensions, the economics may not be viable. Confirm minimum quantity at the RFQ stage.
