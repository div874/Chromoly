How Cut-to-Length Supply Works

The process:
1. Buyer provides a complete cut list: all required OD/wall sizes, lengths, and quantities
2. Supplier verifies the cut list against available stock and provides pricing including cutting cost
3. Buyer places the order with approved cut list
4. Tube is cut from stock, deburred if specified, and packaged for delivery with MTC

The MTC covers all cut pieces from the same heat lot. Cut pieces retain full heat lot traceability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum cut length for 4130 tubing?

Minimum cut lengths depend on the OD, the cutting method, and the supplier's equipment. Very short cuts (below approximately 2 OD lengths) may not be feasible with standard equipment. State your required minimum length at the enquiry stage.
