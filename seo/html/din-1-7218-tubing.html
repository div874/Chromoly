The Werkstoff-Nummer System

Germany's DIN system introduced a numerical material designation (Werkstoff-Nummer) that is now incorporated into the broader European EN system. The number format for alloy steels: 1.XXXX, where the leading '1' identifies the ferrous metals group. The four digits identify the specific alloy sub-group and material within it. 1.7218 is within the 1.7000 group — chromium and chromium-molybdenum steels.

Procurement on German/European Drawings

When ordering against a German drawing specifying 1.7218, buyers should supplement the Werkstoff-Nummer with: the applicable product standard (EN 10297 for seamless mechanical tubing), the required condition suffix, metric dimensions (OD and wall in mm), and the required MTC documentation (EN 10204 3.1). German-drawing procurement also typically specifies tolerances in accordance with the applicable EN standard tolerance class.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 1.7218 the same as 4130?

1.7218 identifies 25CrMo4, which is the European close equivalent of AISI 4130. The alloy family is identical; minor composition limit differences exist between the ASTM and EN specifications. For safety-critical applications, a formal comparison is required before treating them as identical.
