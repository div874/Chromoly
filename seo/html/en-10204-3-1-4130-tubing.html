EN 10204 Certificate Types

EN 10204 defines four main types of inspection documents:

Certificate TypeDescriptionIndependence Level
2.1 — Declaration of conformanceSupplier declares conformance based on non-specific inspectionLow — no test results
2.2 — Test reportSupplier provides test results from non-specific inspectionLow — non-specific testing
3.1 — Inspection certificate (Type A)Manufacturer's authorised representative certifies specific testingMedium — manufacturer internal
3.2 — Inspection certificate (Type B)Certified by manufacturer AND independent body or buyer's repHigh — independent witness

Specifying EN 10204 3.1 on a Purchase Order

To request EN 10204 3.1 documentation, state it explicitly on the purchase order: 'Mill Test Certificate to EN 10204 3.1 required'. This ensures the supplier provides a Type 3.1 certificate — not a lesser 2.2 test report. The MTC should be part of the delivery documentation package and should accompany the material to the point of use.

When 3.2 is Required

EN 10204 3.2 is required by some aerospace OEM programmes, prime contractors, and defence specifications. The additional independent witness adds cost and lead time. Specify 3.2 only if your quality plan or customer requirement explicitly demands it — 3.1 is adequate for most industrial and commercial applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I ask for a 3.1 MTC after the order has been placed?

If the tubing has already been manufactured without 3.1 testing documentation, a retroactive 3.1 certificate cannot be produced — the testing must be performed at the time of manufacture. Always state the MTC requirement at the purchase order stage, not after delivery.
