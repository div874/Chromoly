SCM430 and 4130 — Comparison

SCM430 (JIS G4105) and AISI 4130 (ASTM A519) are close equivalents but are not automatically interchangeable without a formal engineering comparison. The carbon, chromium, and molybdenum limits in JIS G4105 and ASTM A519/A29 are similar but not identical. For procurement where the drawing specifies JIS G4105 SCM430, the substitution of AISI 4130 requires engineering review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I supply AISI 4130 against a JIS G4105 SCM430 requirement?

A substitution requires a formal comparison of the composition limits in JIS G4105 (SCM430) and ASTM A519 (Grade 4130) for the specific heat supplied. If the actual ladle analysis falls within both sets of limits, the substitution may be approved by the responsible engineer. This must be documented and approved — it cannot be assumed.
