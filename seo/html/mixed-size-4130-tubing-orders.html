Benefits of Mixed-Size Consolidated Orders

Consolidating all required 4130 sizes into a single order provides:

1. Unified MTC package: All sizes with their respective MTCs in one documentation set — easier for project records and quality audits.
2. Single delivery point: One shipment rather than multiple arrivals — simplifies receiving inspection.
3. Cost efficiency: Volume across multiple sizes may improve pricing per size.
4. Documentation simplicity: One purchase order, one supplier, one invoice — less administrative overhead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will all sizes in a mixed order come from the same heat lot?

Not necessarily. Different OD/wall sizes are drawn from different heats. Each size will have its own MTC with its own heat lot number. This is normal and acceptable — MTC traceability is maintained per size.
