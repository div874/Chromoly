Why Schedules Don't Apply to 4130 CDS

The pipe schedule system (Schedule 5, 10, 40, 80, 160, etc.) is a wall thickness designation specific to pipe in the NPS system. In the NPS system, a 1" NPS pipe does not have a 1.000" OD — it has a 1.315" OD. The schedule number then defines the wall, not the OD.

4130 chromoly CDS tubing is a mechanical tube: it is sized by actual OD × actual wall. A 1.000" OD × 0.065" wall 4130 CDS tube has an actual OD of 1.000" and an actual wall of 0.065". There is no 'schedule' for this product.

Frequently Asked Questions

If I have a pipe schedule drawing, how do I convert it to 4130 tube specification?

Identify the NPS designation and schedule, then look up the actual OD and wall from an NPS pipe data table. The actual OD will be the OD of the pipe, which you can then specify as the OD of the mechanical tube (if that OD is within the 4130 CDS range). Confirm with the responsible engineer that mechanical tube is appropriate as a substitute for pipe.
