What Schedule 40 Means in Pipe

In the NPS pipe system, Schedule 40 specifies a wall thickness that varies with nominal pipe size. For example, 1" NPS Schedule 40 has OD 1.315" and wall 0.133"; 2" NPS Schedule 40 has OD 2.375" and wall 0.154". These OD values are the actual dimensions used in the pipe system — not the nominal size.

For 4130 mechanical tubing: if the intent is a 4130 tube with dimensions similar to a specific NPS Schedule 40 pipe, the buyer should specify the actual OD and wall (taken from a pipe data table) rather than the schedule designation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I substitute 4130 CDS for Schedule 40 pipe in a pressure application?

Not automatically. Pipe and mechanical tube are different product forms with different governing standards. A formal engineering substitution assessment is required, and the responsible pressure system engineer must confirm acceptability under the applicable pressure code.
