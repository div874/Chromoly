Frequently Asked Questions

Is 4130 CDS tubing available in wall thicknesses matching Schedule 80 pipe?

The Schedule 80 wall thicknesses shown above are within or near the verified wall range (0.035"–0.500") for 4130 CDS. However, the ODs of NPS pipe do not correspond to standard mechanical tube ODs. Confirm specific OD/wall combination availability at the RFQ stage.
