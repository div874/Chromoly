When Stress Relief is Specified

Stress relief is specified for 4130 tubing when:
- The tube has been cold drawn and the residual stresses from drawing may cause dimensional instability during or after machining
- The tube has been welded and the heat-affected zone residual stresses must be reduced without full re-normalising
- A precision component requires dimensional stability and cannot tolerate stress redistribution during machining

For most structural applications (roll cages, airframes), stress relief alone is not specified — normalizing or as-drawn are the standard conditions.

Stress Relief vs. Annealing vs. Normalizing

These three processes achieve different outcomes:

ProcessTemperature RangePrimary Effect
Stress reliefBelow Ac1 (~550–650°C)Reduces residual stress; minimal property change
AnnealingAbove Ac3 (~870–900°C)Full microstructure change — softest, most ductile
NormalizingAbove Ac3, air coolUniform microstructure, intermediate properties
NoteSpecific temperatures depend on the 4130 composition of the heatVerify with heat treater

Frequently Asked Questions

Does stress relief change the mechanical properties of 4130?

Stress relief performed below Ac1 does not significantly change the yield or tensile strength of the material — the microstructure is unchanged. However, ductility (elongation) may improve slightly due to stress relaxation. This is why stress relief is used when properties must be preserved but dimensional stability must be improved.
