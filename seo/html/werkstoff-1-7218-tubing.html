Who Uses the 'Werkstoff' Terminology

The Werkstoff-Nummer system is widely used across Germany, Austria, Switzerland, and European industry more broadly. Engineering drawings from these markets routinely specify material by 1.XXXX number rather than by grade name. Procurement teams, quality managers, and purchasing departments in these regions search for 'Werkstoff 1.7218 tubing' when sourcing from international suppliers, because the Werkstoff-Nr is the , unambiguous identifier on their documentation.

Specifying 1.7218 in International Procurement

When sourcing Werkstoff 1.7218 tubing internationally, include the full specification: Werkstoff-Nr. 1.7218 (25CrMo4), product standard EN 10297, metric dimensions (OD and wall in mm), condition (e.g., +N), and MTC documentation (EN 10204 3.1). International suppliers familiar with European standards will recognise the Werkstoff-Nr immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 'Werkstoff 1.7218' the same as '25CrMo4'?

Yes. 1.7218 is the Werkstoff-Nummer (numerical identifier) for the grade named 25CrMo4 in the EN designation system. They identify the same alloy.
